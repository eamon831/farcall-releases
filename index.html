Farcall

Run your coding agents from your phone.

Every agent, your choice of model, your own computer, no cloud relay.

Like having your terminal's Claude Code or Codex in your pocket, minus the tiny keyboard.

Download How to install

Your agent, your model

Claude Code on your Claude login, or on Z.ai's GLM or Moonshot's Kimi. Codex on OpenAI (preview). Pick per session.

No server in the middle

Your phone reaches your computer over your own private Tailscale network. Your chats and code stay on your machines.

You stay in charge

When an agent wants to run a command or edit a file, your phone asks first. Allow or deny from anywhere.

Ask a panel

Put two or three agents in one chat, read-only. They answer side by side and can discuss each other's answers.

Get started

  1. Install Farcall on your Mac, Windows PC or Linux machine. On a Mac:
    curl -fsSL https://github.com/eamon831/farcall-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
  2. Follow the setup in the Farcall window: Tailscale, Claude Code, a short approval test, your projects folder.
  3. Install the Android app from the QR code in setup, sign in to the same Tailscale account, and pair.
